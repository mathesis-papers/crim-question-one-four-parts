Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

Abstract

For Bašić–Gottlieb–Krnc (2026), Section 5, Question 1, p. 17, we give an alternative proof of Mehat's classification (23 August 2026) of all losing four-part positions in normal-play CRIM, with no bound on the part sizes. For a >= b >= c >= d >= 1, the largest part a must be even; four disjoint cases involving d, c and the parity of b give the remaining conditions. The proof identifies every possible column move by its height and gives an explicit winning move in each complementary case. Consequences include even-rank necessity and hook-stacking closure with at most four rows or columns, the odd-gap winning-move conclusion through six rows or columns using Mehat's rank theorem, exact padding formulas for one- and two-part inputs, and an explanation of 21 of the 38 diagrams in their Appendix A.
